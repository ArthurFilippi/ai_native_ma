Corvina Logistics BV: Confidential Information Memorandum (extract)

Corvina Logistics BV operates temperature-controlled warehouses for pharmaceutical distributors in the Benelux. Revenues for the twelve months to December 2024 amounted to EUR 74.8 million, up from EUR 69.1 million a year earlier.

Corvina employed 612 people at year-end 2024 and operates 9 warehouses with 182,000 pallet positions.

EBITDA margin was 17.2% in 2024. Capex of EUR 6.3 million in 2024 was mostly spent on refrigeration upgrades.

Customer base. Corvina's five biggest clients generated 52% of 2024 revenues; no single client exceeded 15%.
